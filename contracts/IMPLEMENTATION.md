## Planner/comparison version follow-up — 2026-10-23

The integrator-approved `latency-constrained-v2` planner minimizes configured expected variable cost among valid plans satisfying both the absolute 500 ms and relative 1.1× fastest estimated-p95 bounds on the frozen tuning table. Held-out data is not used to choose the plan. Runtime OPA finalization uses a bounded generated-policy computation budget rather than a scheduling-sensitive 50 ms deadline; source transport timeouts and compulsory/charge semantics are unchanged.

New benchmark reports retain `synthetic-v1` observation/policy assumptions and add `manifest.planner_version`. Their `comparisons[].baseline` is the cheapest eligible staged/OPA cost comparator; `latency_baseline`, `latency_baseline_p95_ms` and `latency_p95_limit_ms` separately identify the fastest correct alternative. Optional bounded `disagreements` record requested frozen and effective observed states for measured mismatches, without retries or weakened agreement gates. `source_states_differ` compares full normalized vectors, including legitimately skipped optional positions; it is not a causal classifier for transport versus policy defects. Inspect the retained attempts and outcomes. Older persisted reports retain their original semantics. `Benchmark.report` remains an extensible object, so these optional fields require no request/response breaking change.

See ADR0002/0003 for rationale and limits. The research 20% cost target is independent of execution acceptance and may honestly fail.
